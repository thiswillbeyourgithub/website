I try to collect as little as possible. This site sets no cookies and loads no advertising or tracking scripts.

To see roughly which pages are read, I use [Umami](https://umami.is/), an analytics tool hosted on my own server. It counts visits without identifying visitors: page, referring site, outbound links clicked, browser, device type and approximate country. These statistics stay with me and are not shared.

If you write to me, I only use your message to reply. You can ask me at any time to access or delete what concerns you, through the [contact page](./contact), and you can also contact the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/).
